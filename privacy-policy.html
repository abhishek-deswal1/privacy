Privacy Policy — SnapShare

Last updated: 30 August 2026

SnapShare ("we", "us", or "the app") is an AI-powered event photo-sharing application. Photographers and organizers upload event photos, and guests receive the photos they appear in through automated face matching. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and protect it, the device permissions we request, and the choices you have. By using SnapShare, you agree to the practices described here.

Contact: For any privacy question or data request, email abhishek1010deswal@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and password. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed on our servers.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account.
  • Photos you choose to upload: only the specific event photos and/or the selfie you select. We do not scan, read, or upload your entire photo gallery.
  • Face data (embeddings): to match guests to the photos they appear in, the app generates a numeric mathematical representation ("face embedding") from faces detected in photos. An embedding is a set of numbers, not an image, and is used solely for matching within your events.
  • Device & technical data: a push-notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), an advertising identifier, app version, device model and operating system, and IP address (used for security and fraud prevention).
  • Usage & diagnostics: in-app interactions (analytics) and crash/diagnostic logs, used to keep the app stable and improve it.
  • Advertising data: data used to serve and measure ads, including your advertising identifier and approximate (IP-based) location — see Section 4.

2. Permissions We Request and Why

SnapShare requests each permission only for a specific feature, and only at the point you use it. You can revoke any permission in your device settings at any time.

3. How Your Photos Are Handled

  • We upload only the photos you specifically capture or select. Your gallery is never scanned or uploaded in bulk.
  • Face detection and embedding happen to match guests to photos. Embeddings are numeric vectors used only for matching — we do not use them to identify you outside the app, and we do not sell them.
  • Uploaded photos are stored on secure third-party cloud storage (Cloudinary) and are shared only with verified participants of the same event.
  • You can download photos you are matched with to your own device.

4. Advertising

SnapShare is free and supported by ads. Advertising is served through Google AdMob. AdMob may use mediation to display ads from additional advertising networks, which may include Meta Audience Network and Unity Ads. These ad networks may collect and process your advertising identifier, IP-based approximate location, and device information to show and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted.

You can limit ad personalization by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google's use of advertising data is described in the Google Advertising policies.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party providers that may process some data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — notifications, analytics, and app stability.
  • Google AdMob (and its mediation partners) — advertising, as described in Section 4.
  • Google ML Kit — on-device face detection.
  • Google Sign-In — optional authentication.
  • Cloudinary — secure cloud storage and delivery of event photos.
  • MongoDB Atlas — secure database for account and event data.

Google's handling of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

6. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage your account and events.
  • Detect and match faces so guests receive their photos.
  • Store, deliver, and let you download event photos.
  • Send you notifications about your events.
  • Serve advertisements and measure app performance.
  • Protect the service against abuse and secure your account.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption in transit: all communication between the app and our servers uses encrypted HTTPS/TLS. The app enforces a network security configuration that blocks unencrypted traffic.
  • Secure on-device storage: if you enable "Remember me", your saved login credentials are stored on your device using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences, backed by the hardware-backed Android Keystore.
  • Token-based authentication: we use short-lived access tokens with rotating refresh tokens, so credentials are not repeatedly transmitted.
  • Password protection: passwords are hashed (bcrypt) on our servers and are never stored or logged in plain text.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Photos you upload are shared only with verified participants of the same event. Technical and advertising data may be shared with the service providers and ad networks listed above strictly to operate the app and to serve ads.

9. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account and its associated data (profile, photos, face embeddings, events, and sessions) at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Delete Account, or
  • By emailing abhishek1010deswal@gmail.com.

Account deletion is immediate and irreversible; email requests are processed within 30 days.

10. Children's Privacy

SnapShare is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data using the in-app options or by emailing us. Depending on your region, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
