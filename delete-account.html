Delete Your SnapShare Account

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your SnapShare account and the data associated with it. SnapShare offers two ways to request deletion — directly in the app, or by email.

Option 1: Delete in the app (instant)

  1. Open the SnapShare app and log in.
  2. Tap your profile icon (top left) to open Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete on the warning dialog.

Your account is deleted immediately and permanently — you'll be signed out and returned to the login screen.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address registered on your account:

abhishek1010deswal@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your SnapShare account. We process email deletion requests within 30 days.
